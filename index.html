Proven in the Field

Real deployments, real waterways

From a first-of-its-kind electric fishing vessel to a solar ambulance serving a river-locked community, Seagö's boats are already at work across India.

Case 01
Maharashtra

India's First Electric Fishing Vessel

Built with ICAR-CIFT Mumbai and launched in May 2025, "Vikalpika" is India's first electric fishing vessel — proving zero-emission propulsion for commercial fishing fleets.

Case 02
Kerala

Solar Marine Ambulance

A solar-hybrid marine ambulance serving Kerala's island community — bringing reliable, zero-fuel-cost emergency transport to 17,000+ residents.

Case 03
Karnataka

Tourism Boats — Hampi, Hubli & Gadag

Electric tourism boats deployed across Karnataka's heritage waterways — quiet, exhaust-free rides for visitors to Hampi, Hubli, Gadag, and Bagalkot.
